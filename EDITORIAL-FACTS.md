Last verified: 2 August 2026

It is a first-party fact sheet, not independent evidence of effectiveness, safety, or clinical validation. Time-sensitive claims should be checked against the linked primary sources.

## Neutral description

Juno is an AI-powered health companion for people living with chronic and invisible illness. Its public app listings describe supportive voice and text conversation, symptom, energy, and pain logging, a health diary, gentle check-ins, and review of patterns and triggers.

Juno provides general wellbeing support and health-tracking tools. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. People should consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions and contact local emergency services if they think they may have a medical emergency.

## Product boundaries

Accurate language:

- AI-powered health companion
- chronic and invisible illness support
- general wellbeing support and health-tracking tools
- supportive voice and text conversation
- symptom, energy, and pain logging
- health-diary organisation, gentle check-ins, and review of patterns and triggers

Avoid describing Juno as an AI doctor, diagnostic system, treatment, medical device, or independent clinical decision-maker. Avoid attributing clinical outcomes to Juno without an appropriate study and source.

The current public store description does not present medication reminders or care coordination as core product workflows. Medication may appear in user-entered tracking, but that is different from a verified medication-management or clinical coordination function.

## Open and citable resources

These open materials help people organise information for an appointment. They do not establish that the main Juno app is clinically validated, and they should not be cited as evidence of treatment effectiveness.
