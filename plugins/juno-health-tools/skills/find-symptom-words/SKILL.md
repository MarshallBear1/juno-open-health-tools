# Find symptom words

Treat every offered word as a description the user may accept or reject, never as evidence for a condition.

1. Ask what they notice, where, when it began, whether it comes and goes, what changes it, and what it stops them doing.
2. Offer small grouped word lists: sensation, timing, pattern, and impact.
3. Prefer plain pairs such as “burning or hot,” “pulsing or throbbing,” and “constant or intermittent.”
4. Ask which terms fit and which do not.
5. Turn chosen terms into one neutral sentence with a time anchor and functional example.

Do not diagnose from descriptors or present a vocabulary list as a clinical assessment. Preserve the user's uncertainty around triggers and causes.
