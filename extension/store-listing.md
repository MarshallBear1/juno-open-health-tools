## Summary

Turn symptom observations into a clear, private health appointment plan saved only on your device.

## Description

Juno Appointment Prep helps you capture the details that are easy to forget when an appointment is short: what changed, when it changed, how daily life was affected, and which questions matter most.

Use the toolbar popup to:

- set the goal for your next appointment;
- organise notes as changes, timeline points, impacts, or questions;
- create a plain-language summary;
- copy or download your plan; and
- access a free printable appointment-preparation pack.

Your notes stay in local browser storage unless you choose to copy or download them. Version 1.0.0 has no analytics, ads, account requirement, background tracking, or remote health-data processing.

Juno Appointment Prep is an organisation tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, replace a clinician, or provide emergency care.
