### Mission statement — 75 words maximum

Juno's mission is to make living with chronic illness less confusing and isolating by helping people understand their changing health, preserve the context that is lost between appointments, and communicate what matters to their care team. Juno combines patient-led conversation, longitudinal symptom tracking, accessible summaries and responsible AI boundaries so people can arrive better prepared without presenting the product as a replacement for professional care.

### How does the AI work, why does it matter, who benefits and who pays? — 75 words maximum

Juno builds a longitudinal health profile from a person's conversations, symptom observations, biometrics and medical history. Its AI uses that context to organise changes, surface possible patterns and create appointment-ready summaries. People living with chronic illness benefit because complex histories are hard to reconstruct during short visits. The public app is free to download, with optional consumer subscriptions funding enhanced functionality. Juno supports patient understanding and communication; it does not replace a clinician.

### Measurable outcomes demonstrated or targeted — 75 words maximum

Juno's current reach is more than 150,000 patients globally, and its US App Store listing shows 9,900 ratings at 4.8. Those figures demonstrate adoption and satisfaction, not clinical effectiveness. Evaluation should measure appointment-preparedness scores, completion of stated visit goals, user confidence, clinician-rated summary usefulness, time spent reconstructing history and the accuracy with which reports preserve patient-supplied facts. Diagnosis, treatment and health-outcome claims should wait for appropriately governed studies.

### Does the AI continuously learn from user data? — 75 words maximum

Juno uses each person's evolving health history to provide context-aware support and surface changes over time. The application should describe the production system's model-training and retention practices exactly as implemented rather than implying that every conversation retrains the model. Personalisation, predictive analytics and population-level learning are different processes and should be distinguished. Before submission, confirm which data updates a user's longitudinal profile, which data is retained, and whether any data is used for model improvement.

### Clinical validity, safety and explainability — 75 words maximum

Juno should preserve the user's wording, distinguish reported observations from AI interpretation, communicate uncertainty and avoid presenting possible patterns as definitive diagnoses. Safety design should escalate possible emergencies, encourage appropriate professional care and avoid replacing clinical judgment. Explanations should show which user-supplied facts support a summary or question. The final application must match actual production safeguards, clinical review, privacy controls and incident processes; open tools cannot prove the entire app's compliance.

## One-line description

Juno helps people living with chronic illness turn fragmented symptom experiences into clearer language, timelines, questions, and appointment preparation.

## Short company description

Juno is a patient-led digital health company building practical support for the difficult work that happens between appointments. Its chronic-illness companion helps people capture symptoms, find words for hard-to-describe experiences, notice what has changed, and prepare a concise account for a clinician. Juno is designed to support communication and self-advocacy; it does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.

## Problem

People living with complex or fluctuating chronic illness often arrive at short appointments carrying months or years of fragmented notes, uncertain language, and symptoms that are difficult to describe. Important changes can be lost in the cognitive load of remembering, prioritising, and explaining everything in real time. This communication gap can make already difficult care journeys slower and more stressful for patients and less efficient for clinicians.

## Solution

Juno provides a low-friction, patient-controlled system for capturing observations between visits and turning them into useful appointment inputs: a symptom timeline, concise goals, changes since the last visit, medication and test questions, and plain-language descriptions. The product deliberately separates observation from diagnosis and frames possible patterns as questions to discuss with a clinician.

## Differentiation

Most health trackers optimise for data collection. Juno is designed around the communication bottleneck: helping a person translate messy lived experience into a short, usable conversation. Its patient-led approach combines conversational support, symptom vocabulary, timelines, and appointment preparation while maintaining explicit non-diagnostic boundaries. Juno has also released an open appointment-preparation pack, symptom-diary schema, synthetic examples, and open-source tools so patients and community organisations can benefit without creating an account.

## Patient-led origin

Juno grew from the founding team's lived experience of long, confusing health journeys. Those experiences shaped a product focused on the overlooked layer between symptoms and care: helping people remember what happened, find language for it, and arrive better prepared to be heard. Add personal medical details to the private application only where the person concerned has explicitly approved the wording and audience.

## Impact model

Juno's immediate, measurable outputs are better-organised appointment inputs and greater patient preparedness. The intended downstream value is a clearer patient-clinician conversation with less time spent reconstructing history. Juno does not yet claim diagnosis, treatment, or clinical-outcome improvement. Future evaluation should measure appointment-preparedness scores, user confidence, clinician-perceived usefulness, completion of stated appointment goals, and changes in avoidable repetition or follow-up burden.

## Responsible AI and privacy

Juno is built for a sensitive health context. The product uses explicit non-diagnostic language, avoids inventing causes or missing details, and encourages urgent or emergency care when appropriate. Public examples and open resources use synthetic data. The open tools preserve the user's wording, distinguish observations from interpretations, and are usable without a Juno account.

## Why this category

Juno applies AI directly to a patient-care workflow: preserving longitudinal context, helping a person organise observations, and preparing information for a clinical conversation. AI in Patient Care is therefore more defensible than Home Health Diagnostic & Monitoring, which could imply remote diagnosis or provider monitoring that the evidence here does not establish.
