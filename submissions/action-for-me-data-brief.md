## Email copy

Hello Action for M.E. / PRIME team,

I’m Marshall Gould, co-founder of Juno. I live with ME/CFS, and Juno grew from the difficulty of turning fluctuating, hard-to-describe experiences into a useful conversation during a short appointment.

I saw that PRIME is inviting researchers and industry partners to discuss patient and public involvement. I’d like to explore a small, governance-first collaboration: a co-authored public data brief on what people with ME/CFS struggle to communicate before and during appointments, which low-burden preparation supports are genuinely usable, and what patients want clinicians to ask or acknowledge.

I’ve attached a three-page concept note. The proposal deliberately starts with classification and governance before any data collection. It rules out patient records, direct identifiers, commercial lead generation, and publication without shared interpretation and approval. No participant data has been collected. The immediate ask is simply a 30-minute conversation to decide whether the question is useful and what route would be proportionate.

Juno can contribute the initial drafting, open templates, accessible prototypes, analysis support under an agreed governance structure, and publication production. We would look to Action for M.E. / PRIME for lived-experience governance, accessibility and safeguarding guidance, contributor relationships, interpretation, and co-authorship.

Open materials:

- https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21730086
- https://github.com/MarshallBear1/juno-open-health-tools

If this sits better with another member of your team, I’d be grateful if you could point me in the right direction.

Best,

Marshall Gould<br>
Co-founder, Juno<br>
https://junocompanion.com/

## Concept-note boundaries

- Discussion only: not a research protocol, recruitment notice, medical advice, or claim of clinical effectiveness.
- No participant data has been collected.
- Classification, governance, ethics, data protection, and controller responsibilities must be agreed before recruitment.
- No patient records, direct identifiers, commercial lead generation, or participant-level publication.
- Action for M.E. / PRIME and lived-experience contributors would share interpretation and publication approval.
