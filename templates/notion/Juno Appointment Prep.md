# Juno Appointment Prep

> A private, practical workspace for organising what you want to discuss at a health appointment. This template does not diagnose, recommend treatment, or replace a clinician.

## Start here

- [ ] Write the one outcome that would make the appointment useful.
- [ ] Choose the three changes or observations that matter most.
- [ ] Note what is different from your usual baseline.
- [ ] Check your current medicines and supplements against authoritative labels or records.
- [ ] Put your three most important questions in order.

## During the appointment

- Start with the main goal.
- Use concrete examples and time ranges.
- Ask what the next step is, who owns it, and when to follow up.
- Ask how to seek help if things change before then.

## After the appointment

- [ ] Write what was agreed in your own words.
- [ ] Check unclear instructions with the care team.
- [ ] Record follow-up dates and tasks.
- [ ] Keep only the information that remains useful.

- **Open questions:** `Status` is `Open`.

## Privacy note

Notion is a general-purpose workspace, not a clinical record system. Decide whether it is appropriate for the sensitivity of information you plan to add, keep the page private by default, review sharing permissions, and avoid unnecessary identifiers.
