# Appointment checklist

## A few days before

- Pick the one outcome that would make this appointment useful.
- Choose the three changes or observations that matter most.
- Note what you can no longer do, do less often, or need help doing.
- Reconcile your current medicines and supplements from authoritative labels or records.
- Write three questions in priority order.

## During

- Start with the main goal.
- Use concrete examples and time ranges.
- Ask what the next step is, who owns it, and when to follow up.
- Ask how to seek help if things change before then.

## After

- Write down what was agreed in your own words.
- Check any unclear instruction with the care team.
- Record follow-up dates and tasks.

This checklist organises a conversation; it does not provide medical advice.
