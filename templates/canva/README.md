# Juno Appointment Prep — editable Canva import

`Juno Appointment Prep - Editable Canva Import.pptx` is an eight-page, layered appointment-preparation template. Text, rules, checkboxes, table cells, panels, and headings are separate editable objects rather than a single flattened image.

## Import into Canva

1. Download the `.pptx` file from this folder.
2. In Canva, choose **Create a design** and then **Import file**.
3. Select the downloaded PowerPoint.
4. Open the imported design and immediately choose **File → Make a copy** before adding personal information.
5. Rename the copy for the appointment. Delete any sections that are not useful.

Canva may substitute a font or shift a line slightly during import. The template uses Arial and simple native shapes to minimise those changes.

## Pages

1. Cover and privacy reminder
2. Appointment goal and three useful prompts
3. What changed since the last visit
4. Observable symptom descriptions
5. Simple timeline
6. Priority questions
7. After-visit plan
8. Safety and scope

## Privacy and safety

Keep the blank original separate from any personal copy. Review Canva's privacy settings before adding health information, and avoid adding information you would not want handled by the service.

The template helps organise user-supplied information. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, replace a clinician, or provide emergency care. If symptoms may be urgent or life-threatening, contact local emergency services or an appropriate clinician.
